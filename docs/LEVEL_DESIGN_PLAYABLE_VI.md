# Thiet Ke Man Choi (Playable)

Tai lieu nay tom tat 6 man choi co the dua thang vao du an hien tai.
Muc tieu la tao progression ro rang: hoc co ban -> sinh ton -> combat cao -> mini boss.

## Nguyen tac chung
- M1-M2 day nguoi choi nhap mon va thu thap.
- M3-M4 day xung dot giua farm tai nguyen va giao tranh.
- M5-M6 day ap luc sinh ton ban dem + muc tieu combat.
- Moi man chi co 1-2 objective chinh de tranh roi.

## Man 1 - Bat dau
- Ban do: `level_01/map.tmx`
- Muc tieu:
  - Thu `wood x10`
  - Song sot 60 giay
- Enemy:
  - 1-2 orc vao ban dem
- Resource:
  - Cay day quanh khu spawn
  - Da it, dung de gioi thieu

## Man 2 - Cham tran
- Ban do: `level_02/map.tmx`
- Muc tieu:
  - Tieu diet `3` quai
  - Thu `wood x8`
- Enemy:
  - Orc xuat hien som hon
- Resource:
  - Cay/da chia 2 cum de buoc nguoi choi di chuyen

## Man 3 - Duy tri
- Ban do: `level_03/map.tmx`
- Muc tieu:
  - Thu `wood x15`
  - Thu `stone x10`
- Enemy:
  - 2 loai orc + skeleton vao chieu/toi
- Resource:
  - Dat da xa hon cay de tao lua chon rui ro

## Man 4 - Vong vay
- Ban do: `level_04/map.tmx`
- Muc tieu:
  - Song sot 180 giay
  - Tieu diet `5` quai
- Enemy:
  - Spawn nhip deu quanh ria map
- Resource:
  - It tai nguyen, tap trung cho combat

## Man 5 - Ap luc
- Ban do: `level_05/map.tmx`
- Muc tieu:
  - Thu tong cong `25` tai nguyen bat ky
  - Tieu diet `8` quai
- Enemy:
  - Ban dem tang manh mat do spawn
- Resource:
  - Them carrot/food de bu nang luong

## Man 6 - Mini Boss
- Ban do: `level_06/map.tmx`
- Muc tieu:
  - Tieu diet `1` mini boss (orc HP cao)
  - Song sot den het dem dau tien
- Enemy:
  - 1 mini boss + quai thuong ho tro
- Resource:
  - Vua du wood/stone de khong bi ket energy

## Can bang nhanh
- Damage nguoi choi co ban: 1, skill F: 2.
- HP goi y:
  - Orc: 5-7
  - Skeleton: 5-6
  - Mini boss: 20-25
- Neu test thay qua kho:
  - Giam 15-20% toc do spawn dem
  - Tang drop food +1 moi cum resource lon
